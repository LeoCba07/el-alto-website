## What changed

### Hero enquiry widget (`cc38d42`)

- **Solid cream, no blur.** At 90% with `backdrop-blur-md`, the photo tinted
  the card a little differently on every slide, and it read as grey rather
  than glass.
- **Consultar is brand forest,** with the WhatsApp glyph in its green. White
  on `#25D366` is about 2:1, below AA even for large text. The focus outline
  is amber, since white vanished against the cream card.
- **No icons in the labels.** The date inputs draw their own calendar glyph,
  so each cell showed two.
- **Phones keep the tight labels** (0.65rem, 0.06em). Larger labels widened
  "Personas" until "Elegir fecha" clipped against the calendar glyph at
  360px. Cell padding went from `px-2.5` to `px-2` below `sm`. Room left after
  the hint: 22px at 360px, 37px at 390px.

### Hero copy (`cc38d42`)

- **The "Desde 1996 · A 20 minutos…" caps line is gone.** The kicker carries
  place and age, so the hero had two competing caps lines.
- **Kicker:** 12px on phones and 14px from `sm`, tracking 0.08em / 0.18em,
  `text-white/85`. The old 0.3em tracking read as template luxury.
- **Description:** regular weight instead of light, which dissolved into the
  photo; `max-w-2xl` and a soft text shadow. Merriweather only ships 700, so
  it stays in Geist rather than adding a font weight.
- **Line breaks:** each comma-separated clause is an `inline-block`, so a
  phone breaks "Tranquilidad serrana, / a 20 minutos…" instead of "…a 20 /
  minutos". A clause too long for the line still wraps inside itself.
- **Final wording,** both from Sanity's Hero section:
  - tagline: `Tanti · Sierras de Córdoba · Desde 1996`
  - description: `Tranquilidad serrana, a 20 minutos de Villa Carlos Paz`

  The code defaults match, for when Sanity is empty.

## Tooling notes

- **A hidden Browser pane renders no frames.** Screenshots at the top of the
  page work. After a programmatic scroll they come back blank. With no frames:
  - `requestAnimationFrame`, `scroll` events and `IntersectionObserver` don't
    fire
  - CSS transitions freeze mid-way, so the widget's button showed its old
    green, and counters stopped at values like "0.3/5"
- **The workaround used today:**
  - hide the sections above the one being inspected with injected CSS
  - force `[style*="opacity: 0"]` reveals visible
  - turn transitions off
  - dispatch `scroll` by hand
- **The page wrapper is `div.min-h-screen`.** With every section hidden, it
  keeps a screen-tall blank block, which looked like a gap before the footer.
  There's none on the real page: the final CTA ends where the footer starts.
- **`.claude/launch.json`** (untracked) gained `dev-variants`: `next dev` on
  port 3002. Next 16 builds dev into `.next/dev`, so it doesn't touch the
  production build that `prod-local` serves, and the usual dev server on 3000
  stays free.

## 12 September: enquiries without dates

Leandro's call, before sending Iván the preview: guests, often older ones,
press Consultar without picking dates, and the error plus the shake stopped
them at the one moment they had decided to write. Now the enquiry goes out
with whatever is there:

| Dates picked | What WhatsApp gets | `fechas` in `whatsapp_click` |
|---|---|---|
| None | The floating button's generic message (`DEFAULT_MESSAGE`, now exported from `WhatsAppButton`) | `ninguna` |
| Only one | That date and the guest count | `una` |
| Both | Both dates and the guest count, as before | `ambas` |

Only dates that can't be right still stop it with an error and the shake: a
past check-in, or a check-out not after the check-in. They would reach the
owner as a wrong enquiry. The pickers' `min` already rules both out, so it's
rare.

The `fechas` field lets GA4 show how many enquiries skip the dates. All five
cases were checked in the browser with `window.open` and `gtag` stubbed; the
shake itself couldn't be seen in the hidden pane, and its code didn't change.
