## Context

Three separate threads ran into each other today.

**1. The domain was still half-hosted elsewhere — resolved today.**
`complejoelalto.com.ar` pointed at a cPanel box at datawebhosting whose only
remaining job was answering DNS, on an annual subscription expiring next month.
Because the whole zone lived there, letting it lapse would have taken `www` down
too, not just the apex — Vercel served the site, but datawebhosting served the
record that pointed at Vercel.

The nameservers now point at Vercel and it is verified end to end: delegation at
the `.ar` registry, apex redirecting to `www` under a valid certificate, the
Search Console TXT record carried across, and no trace of the old host left in
the zone. The apex had also been serving a certificate issued for
`ftp.complejoelalto.com.ar`, so anyone typing the bare domain got a full-page
security warning; that went away with the move. Nothing about the old hosting
account needs preserving — it held no files, no mail in use, nothing.

**2. The owner ran the site through Gemini** and forwarded two audits. Roughly
half of what came back was already done, some was wrong, and a few items were
real and serious. Sorting which was which drove most of the code work.

**3. The owner asked for a quote** on a restyle plus videos. The scoping
conversation surfaced decisions that changed what was worth building — most
notably that prices deserved their own page, and that a capacity filter did not.

The through-line: the site had a Sanity CMS wired up, but a lot of content was
hardcoded in components alongside it. The two drifted. Several of today's bugs
are the same bug — the CMS said one thing and the code said another.

One standing decision worth recording rather than rediscovering: the site stays
on Vercel's free tier. That tier is nominally non-commercial, and this is a
business, so it is a known and accepted risk — enforcement at this traffic level
is essentially unheard of, and the realistic worst case is an email asking for an
upgrade. Adding video changes the calculation, which is why the December plan
keeps video off Vercel's bandwidth: YouTube for the unit clips, and a small
self-hosted loop for the hero.

## What remains

### Ready to build

- **Hero enquiry bar — open the date picker from the digits, not just the
  icon.** A native `<input type="date">` only opens its picker when the little
  calendar glyph is clicked; clicking the numbers drops the reader into
  typing mode. So the widget currently reads as though it wants the date typed
  in by hand, until someone happens to notice the icon. `input.showPicker()`
  fixes it, called from the click or focus handler — it has to run inside a
  user gesture, and it is worth checking on Safari and Firefox, which have
  historically differed here.
- **Hero enquiry bar — make the card slightly transparent.** It is currently
  solid `bg-cream/95`. Worth watching contrast on the text and inputs as it
  gets lighter, since the photo behind it changes every few seconds with the
  carousel, and a crop that works over the pool shot may not over the sierras
  one.
